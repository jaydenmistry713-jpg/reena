Our Services

Authentic Punjabi entertainment for weddings, Sangeet nights, melas and private events — in the UK and worldwide.

  1. Home
  2. Services

We provide five services, and most events combine more than one. Each has its own page covering what is included, how it works and what to expect — and booking them together means the handovers on the night are planned rather than improvised.

Ladies Sangeet

Female singers and performers who lead the whole evening, with live dholki and the traditional boliyan that get both families joining in.

Punjabi Folk Singers

Live traditional vocals for Sangeet nights, receptions and melas — Punjabi folk, Pakistani classics, Bollywood and modern fusion.

Dhol Players

Live dhol for baraat entries, couple entrances and dance sets — the sound that turns an arrival into an entrance.

Punjabi DJ Services
