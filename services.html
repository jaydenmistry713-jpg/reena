Punjabi Entertainment Services

Authentic Punjabi entertainment for weddings, Sangeet nights, melas, cultural celebrations, private events and more — across the UK and internationally.

  1. Home
  2. Services

Our work falls into two kinds of booking, and they are genuinely different jobs. Weddings run across several events in a week and need the handovers between performers planned. Concerts, melas and cultural events are a single slot on somebody else’s bill, with a stage, a sound provider and other acts to work around. Everything below sits under one or the other.

Our main pillar

Punjabi Wedding Entertainment

A Punjabi wedding is several events, not one. The Sangeet, Mehndi, Jago, baraat and reception each need something different, and booking them together means the handovers are planned rather than improvised. Start here if you are planning a wedding — it covers the whole week and which of the four performer types each night calls for.
