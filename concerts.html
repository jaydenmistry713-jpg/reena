Concert & Mela Performances

Full live line-ups for melas, festivals and community events — scaled to the stage you have.

We provide full live Punjabi concert line-ups — singers, dhol players and musicians — for melas, cultural festivals, community events and private concerts. Line-ups scale from a community hall to a festival main stage, and the team is assembled for the booking.

Built for the stage you actually have

A concert booking is a different problem from a wedding booking. The organiser usually has a stage, a slot length, a sound provider and a bill of other acts to work around, and the performance has to fit all four.

That is why the line-up is assembled per event rather than offered as a fixed package. A community hall and a festival main stage need different things, and we scale to what is in front of us.

What a concert line-up can include

  • ✓Lead vocals from Reena Kaur, with over twenty-five years of live performance.
  • ✓Supporting singers and performers, drawn from our team.
  • ✓Live dhol players.
  • ✓Musicians, as the set requires.
  • ✓Coordination with your production and sound team on stage requirements and running order.

Events we perform at

Melas and cultural festivals, community and charity events, civic and council-run programmes, corporate functions, and private concerts. Reena Kaur has performed at events including Wolverhampton’s Summer Mela.

Why a live line-up rather than a single act

A solo vocalist with backing tracks is a cheaper booking, and for some slots it is the right one. But a festival crowd standing in a field responds to a band in a way it does not respond to a backing track, and the difference is most obvious in the moments between songs — when a live dhol can hold a crowd that a silent gap would lose.

A full line-up also gives you flexibility on the day. If your schedule moves, a live band can extend or compress. A fixed backing track cannot.

For event organisers

If you are programming a bill, the practical questions — slot length, stage plot, sound requirements, arrival times — are best handled directly. Send the details of the event and we will come back with what the line-up needs.

We are used to working to someone else’s production schedule and to sharing a stage and a sound provider with other acts, which is the normal situation at a mela. Tell us who your sound provider is and we will deal with them directly on the technical side.

Multi-date and touring bookings can be discussed the same way.

Travel

We travel across the UK and internationally. Travel costs are worked into the quote up front, so the figure you are given is the figure you pay.

Your line-up

The line-up is chosen for the booking rather than taken off a shelf. We bring as many performers as the event calls for, and the team is picked to suit the style and scale of your celebration.

Pricing and booking

Every event is quoted individually. Pricing is bespoke and based on what your day actually needs — the line-up, the running order and the travel involved — so there is no fixed price list. Tell us what you are planning and we will put a quote together for it.

For weddings and major events we recommend booking around twelve months ahead, since prime dates go early. Shorter notice is always worth asking about — we accommodate last-minute bookings wherever availability allows.

Frequently asked questions

What size events do you perform at?

Everything from community halls to festival main stages. The line-up is assembled for the booking, so it scales to the venue and the slot rather than being a fixed package.

Who performs in a concert line-up?

Lead vocals from Reena Kaur, with supporting singers and performers, live dhol players and musicians as the set requires. The exact line-up is agreed with you.

Do you work with our sound and production team?

Yes. We coordinate stage requirements and running order with your production and sound provider ahead of the event.

Do you take international concert bookings?

Yes. We perform across the UK and internationally, with travel costs built into the quote.

Can you perform multiple dates?

Yes, multi-date and touring bookings can be arranged. Get in touch with the dates and venues.

How do we book a performance for our festival?

Send the event details — date, venue, slot length and what else is on the bill — through the contact form and we will come back with a quote and what the line-up needs.

Ready to talk about your event?

Tell us the date, the venue and what you have in mind, and we will put together a bespoke quote.
