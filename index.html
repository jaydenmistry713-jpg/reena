Meet Reena Kaur

Twenty-five years of keeping Punjabi folk alive

Reena Kaur is a Punjabi singer and the founder of Punjabi Folk Entertainment, providing live Punjabi music and entertainment for weddings, Sangeet ceremonies, cultural events and private celebrations across the UK and internationally.

Over 25 years of dedication have gone into preserving and celebrating the vibrant heritage of Punjabi culture, and every booking is led personally.

Our mission is to keep the essence of Punjabi folk traditions alive, bringing authentic, soulful experiences to your events and celebrations.

Whether you’re planning a wedding, birthday, or any special occasion, we offer a variety of services, from skilled singers and energetic dhol players to dynamic DJs, creating an unforgettable atmosphere.
