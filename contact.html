Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 12 months in advance for weddings and major events. However, we understand last-minute needs and will do our best to accommodate your request.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in the United Kingdom and serve clients worldwide. We travel internationally to bring authentic Punjabi entertainment to your celebration, wherever you are. Contact us to discuss your event location.

Can we customise the music selection?

Absolutely! We work closely with you to understand your preferences and create a customised playlist that perfectly matches your event's atmosphere.
